Who we are
Fallow land is not wasteland.
It is land resting — recovering,
holding memory, waiting.

We believe every degraded acre
still knows what it once was.
Earth Fallow Alliance works at the intersection of ecology and people. We plant trees where forests once stood, carry rural voices into policy rooms that rarely hear them, rethink how communities handle what they discard, and give young people the tools to think like stewards — not just consumers.
Thematic Areas

Four lenses.
One mission.

Everything we do falls within one of these four interconnected thematic areas. Together, they form our whole approach to environmental renewal.

01
🌳
Reforestation & Land Restoration

We work with communities to restore degraded land through tree planting, soil regeneration, and long-term stewardship — returning ecological memory to places that have forgotten what they once were.

Active in 22 sites
02
📢
Climate Advocacy & Policy

We ensure that rural and indigenous voices reach national and regional policy tables. Through community evidence, briefs, and campaigns, we hold decision-makers accountable to the land and its people.

10 Policy engagements
03
♻️
Waste Reduction & Circular Economy

We pilot waste-free systems in real markets and communities — building composting networks, upcycling hubs, and producer accountability frameworks that reduce environmental burden at the source.

6 Community pilots
04
🎓
Environmental Education & Youth

Through our fellowship, ambassador programme, and school partnerships, we equip the next generation with the knowledge, tools, and confidence to lead environmental stewardship in their own communities.

6,800 youth trained
What we stand for

Principles we
never compromise.

04
01
Community First, Always

We never design a programme without the people it serves. Every intervention is co-created. We arrive with questions, not blueprints.

02
Science-Led Action

Every initiative we run is grounded in ecological evidence. We measure, report, and adapt. Good intentions are not enough — outcomes matter.

03
Independence & Integrity

We are community-funded. No corporate interest shapes our work. Our accountability runs to the land and to the people who depend on it.

04
Long-Term Thinking

Forests take decades. So does real community change. We are not here for quick wins. We commit to slow, necessary, generational work.

Programs

Rooted in
real places.

Every programme is co-designed with the communities it serves. We arrive with questions. Everything that follows is built from the answers.

01
Restoration
The Fallow Fields Initiative
Community-led tree planting and soil regeneration in degraded agricultural zones. We train local planters, establish seed banks, and stay for the long run.
02
Advocacy
The Community Climate Desk
Policy briefs, public campaigns, and direct community representation at national and regional environmental bodies.
03
Circular Economy
Zero-to-Landfill Pilots
Working with local markets and producers to test waste-free supply chains, composting networks, and upcycling hubs.
04
Education
Green Futures Fellowship
An annual fellowship for 18–25 year olds to design and implement a local environmental project with full mentorship and funding.
05
Community
Alliance Ambassadors
A volunteer network of local champions embedding environmental practice into markets, schools, and community gatherings.
06
Research
The Land Memory Project
Documenting ecological change at a hyper-local level — pairing universities with elders who remember what the land once looked like.
